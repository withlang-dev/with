# Open a window and draw in it

**The task, as a user brings it.** "Open a window, draw a shape that moves, and close cleanly when I press Escape or close the window."

**The library they reach for.** raylib (`with get c.raylib`) or SDL (`with get c.sdl`).

**Done when** the window opens, animates and closes without a crash or a leak report, on a machine with a display. The check that the picture is right is a human one (#3013).

**Platforms.** uat/README.md lists where each of the two builds.
